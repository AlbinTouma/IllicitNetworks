Our mission

Illicit Networks is a comprehensive site that curates information about illicit actors and their networks. Our mission is to collect comprehensive intelligence about illicit actors and their networks and to make this information public.

In this sense, our approach is different from notable sources such as OpenSanctions, EveryPolitician that make public data in large quantities to the public domain. It is our hope that by curating profiles we can enrich and compliment large sources and help investigative journalists, banks, and law enforcement cut through the noise.
